First Time Guest

We’re glad you’re here.

A few things that might help today, and a way to say hello.

  • About 70 minutesWorship and practical Bible teaching
  • Come as you areNo need to dress up
  • Kids welcomeCheck in to Kid Zone before service
  • Free coffeeGrab a cup and settle in
How long does it last?

About 70 minutes. Roughly 20 of those are worship in song, followed by announcements, a teaching from the Bible, and communion. Prayer teams are available at the end if you’d like someone to pray with you.

What about my kids?

Check in to Kid Zone before service and our team will help you find the right classroom.

Will I be asked to give money?

No. Giving is always your choice, and there’s no expectation for guests.

Let us know you were here

Tell us a little about yourself and someone from our Edina team will be in touch.
