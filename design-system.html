Christian Family Church

Quiet frame,
loud photography.

The interface is cream, slate and one caramel accent in a single typeface, so the photos and video you embed do the work. Layouts and content order are unchanged from the wireframe.

1 · Photo first

Every page opens with a full-bleed photo or video. Text sits bottom-left on a soft shade.

2 · One typeface

Inter Tight in two weights. Headlines are tight and large; the em half of a headline fades to taupe.

3 · One accent

Caramel is a dot, a hover, a focus ring, and the Give band. It never carries body text.

4 · Pills + hairlines

Buttons are pills. Lists and cards are separated by 1px lines, not boxes.

Color

Sampled from the brick-color reference: cream and peach neutrals, caramel from the brown rows, slate from the top-right corner. Rhythm down a page: cream → peach → slate → cream → peach.

Layout, shape, motion

Content is capped at 1360px and padded by --gutter. Sections breathe with --section (72px mobile → 150px desktop).

Space

--gutter 22 → 80px
--section 72 → 150px
--header-h 76px (68 mobile)

Shape

--radius 16px (photos, cards)
--radius-pill 999px (buttons, chips)
Lines: 1px --line

Motion

Entrance: fade + rise 26px, .9s
State: header goes solid on scroll; image zoom 4.5% on card hover
Feedback: buttons lift 2px; arrows nudge
All off for reduced-motion
