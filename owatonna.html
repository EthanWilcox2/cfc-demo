Welcome to CFC Owatonna

Sunday worship · 9:00 AM

Before you come

Good questions.

The things first-time guests ask us most. Anything else? Call or email and we’ll help.

Will I be singled out?

No. Friendly people will greet you at the door, and we promise not to ask you to stand, speak, or do anything that makes you uncomfortable. Find a seat and enjoy the service.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. We’re interested in you, not how you dress. Many people wear jeans, some dress up more, and we want you to just be you.

What is the service like?

Relaxed and friendly, with contemporary worship music and practical Bible teaching. Sing and take part in whatever way feels comfortable to you.

How long does it last?

About 70 minutes. Roughly 20 of those are worship in song, followed by announcements, a teaching from the Bible, and communion. Prayer teams are available at the end if you’d like someone to pray with you.

What about my kids?

Check in to Kid Zone before service and our team will help you find the right classroom. You can read about each age group in the kids and teens section below.

Is the building accessible?

Yes, our building is accessible. If you have any questions about getting in or around, call or email and we’ll help.

Will I be asked to give money?

No. Giving is always your choice, and there’s no expectation for guests.

Do I need to tell you I’m coming?

Not at all. You’re welcome to just show up. If you’d like someone to watch for you or answer questions first, let us know here, or call 507 455 3205.
